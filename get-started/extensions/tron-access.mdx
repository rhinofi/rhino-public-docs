title: "Tron, Solana and Bitcoin Access"
description: "Enable transactions on Tron, Solana and Bitcoin where there are higher onchain costs"
---

**Extension tier:** Premium

Tron, Solana and Bitcoin all have higher onchain transaction costs and as such access to these chains must be enabled through this extension. 

---

## What's enabled

- **Deposits to a Tron Smart Deposit Address** — Smart Deposit Addresses can be generated on Tron. Users can send [supported tokens](https://docs.rhino.fi/get-started/supported-chains) to a Rhino.fi SDA on Tron, and funds are settled to the configured destination chain. For all deposits to a Tron Smart Deposit Address there is a per-transaction \$1.40 cost.
- **Tron as a destination** — Bridges can settle to a Tron address. There is a per-transaction \$1.40 cost for this.
- **Solana as a destination** — Bridges can settle to a Solana address. There is a per-transaction \$0.50 cost for this.
- **Deposits to a Bitcoin Smart Deposit Address** — Smart Deposit Addresses can be generated on Bitcoin. Users can send [supported tokens](https://docs.rhino.fi/get-started/supported-chains) to a Rhino.fi SDA on Bitcoin, and funds are settled to the configured destination chain. For all deposits to a Bitcoin Smart Deposit Address there is a per-transaction \$1 plus 20bps cost.

---

## Why this is a separate extension

These chains requires specific compliance and operational controls that differ from EVM chains. AML screening on TRON operates differently from EVM screening, and Rhino maintains dedicated TRON infrastructure.

Enabling TRON access therefore requires a specific agreement with your account representative, including acknowledgement of the TRON-specific compliance framework.

---

## Integration

Once Tron Access is enabled on your account, TRON appears in the bridge config (`bridgeConfigs`) as an available chain with `enabledDepositAddress: true`.

No code changes are required beyond confirming TRON is listed.

For SDA generation on TRON, use `"TRON"` in the `depositChains` array.

<Info>
  Generating a Tron (and Solana) SDA requires a separate call. I.e. you can specify many EVMs to generate SDAs across all EVMs. Then generate a Tron (and Solana) SDA with a separate call.
</Info>

For TRON as a destination, use `"TRON"` as the `destinationChain`.

Token symbols on TRON follow the same naming convention as other chains (e.g. `"USDT"`).
